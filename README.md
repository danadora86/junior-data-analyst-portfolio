# Junior Data Analyst Portfolio

**A practical portfolio demonstrating data analysis, SQL, Python, Microsoft Fabric and Power BI through real-world business projects.**

## About

I am a Computer Science student developing my skills in data analytics and business intelligence, with a focus on turning raw data into meaningful insights that support business decisions.

This portfolio documents my practical learning and project work, using real datasets and business scenarios. It demonstrates my approach to data preparation, analysis, reporting and communicating findings.

## Technical Skills

The portfolio will demonstrate practical work with:

- **SQL:** Data exploration, querying, joins, aggregations, CTEs and window functions.
- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, data cleaning, analysis and visualisation.
- **Microsoft Fabric:** Lakehouses, data pipelines, notebooks and data warehousing.
- **Power BI:** Data modelling, dashboards, KPIs and business reporting.
- **Analytics:** Data quality, trend analysis, customer insights and business recommendations.

*Skills and tools will be updated as projects are completed.*

## How I Approach Projects
